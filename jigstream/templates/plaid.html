How it works

Plaid is the service most finance apps use to read bank and card activity. You make your own free Plaid developer account, so you get your own free allowance and nothing is shared with anyone. You sign in to your bank in Plaid's window; BotTrack never sees your bank password. Your Plaid keys and bank link are stored encrypted on this Mac.

If you'd rather not, importing statement files on the Bank page does the same matching for free with no account.

1. Get your free Plaid keys

  1. Go to dashboard.plaid.com/signup and create an account (choose "personal use" or your own name as the company if asked).
  2. Open Developers → Keys. Copy the client_id and the Sandbox secret.
  3. Paste them below with Sandbox chosen and press Save. In Sandbox you can try the whole flow with Plaid's test bank (username user_good, password pass_good).
  4. For your real bank, ask for Production access in the Plaid dashboard (the free tier for a few of your own accounts is enough; Plaid may ask a few questions about your use). When it is approved, copy the Production secret, paste it below with Production chosen, and link your banks.
