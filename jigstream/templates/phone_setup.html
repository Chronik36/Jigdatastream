Phone access

See and edit your orders, expenses and profiles from your iPhone or Android phone. Your phone talks straight to this Mac, so nothing goes through anyone's server, and it only works while BotTrack is open here.

  1. Install the free Tailscale app on this Mac and on your phone, and sign in to both with the same account. It connects them privately and works away from home.
  2. Turn phone access on below, choose the email and password you'll sign in with, then restart BotTrack. macOS may ask whether BotTrack can accept incoming connections: choose Allow.
  3. On your phone, open the address shown below in Safari or Chrome, sign in, and use Add to Home Screen.
