Sign in to a provider's dashboard

For providers with no API. BotTrack opens their site in a window; sign in there yourself (Discord works), then generate a proxy list once the way you normally do. BotTrack watches how the page makes it, and Refresh pool repeats that for you. The window stays signed in until BotTrack closes; after a restart, press Sign in again on the account.

Some providers' terms don't allow automating their dashboard; use this at your own judgment for each one.
