Sign in to a provider's dashboard

For providers with no API. BotTrack opens their site in a window; sign in there yourself (Discord works), then generate a proxy list once the way you normally do. BotTrack watches how the page makes it, and Refresh pool repeats that for you. The sign-in is kept on this Mac, encrypted, so you only sign in again when the site itself signs you out.

Some providers' terms don't allow automating their dashboard; use this at your own judgment for each one.

Shared steps

The steps you taught for each session type and TTL can help everyone on the same provider: when they pick that choice, BotTrack can do your steps in their window for them. Only the motions are shared: the provider's site, which control you used (its words and place on the page) and what you picked, plus minutes you typed. Never sign-ins, cookies, tokens, list addresses, proxies or anything else you typed.
