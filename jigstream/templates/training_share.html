Shared learning

Every correction you make teaches your own BotTrack. Sharing teaches everyone's, without sharing your emails: only how the AI learned leaves this Mac. That means which store, what the right answer was, and which shopping words or labels gave it away. Lessons from all users are checked, merged and ship with updates, and each copy shows its model the lessons for the store an email came from.

Share what your corrections taught

A lesson holds only the store's email domain, the answer from BotTrack's own lists (order, shipped, botting expense…), shopping words from the subject (like "your order" or "has shipped"), and the label in front of an order number, tracking number or total (like "Order #"). It never holds email text, names, addresses, numbers, amounts, products or dates. Words that aren't on BotTrack's fixed lists are dropped, so nothing of yours can slip through.
