Turn on FileVault to use BotTrack

FileVault is required. BotTrack keeps your Gmail app passwords, your card numbers, store logins, proxy accounts and every order and address on this Mac. Without FileVault, anyone who gets your Mac, or just its drive (lost, stolen, sold, or in for repair), can copy all of that without knowing your password. With FileVault on, the disk is unreadable without your Mac password.

It's free, built into macOS, and you only set it up once. Your orders and settings stay exactly as they are, and BotTrack opens by itself as soon as FileVault is on.

  1. Press Open System Settings below (or open System Settings, then Privacy & Security).
  2. Scroll to FileVault and press Turn On…, then enter your Mac password.
  3. Choose how to unlock your disk if you forget your password: your Apple Account, or a recovery key. Write the recovery key down and keep it somewhere safe.
  4. Restart if macOS asks. Encryption keeps running in the background while you use the Mac.
