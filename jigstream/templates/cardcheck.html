What your cards say really went through. For each card on the Bank page, charges at a store are grouped by their exact amount and the dates close together (one drop). A refund of the same amount on the same card takes one away, so went through is charges minus refunds. The amount tells what was bought: an order email with that total, or a bot checkout whose price plus tax and shipping comes to it. A charge is only tied to one checkout when that's certain: the bank gave the charge a time within {{minutes}} minutes of the checkout, it paid that checkout's order email, or it's the only charge and the only checkout. Otherwise you see counts, not which one is which. A tied charge that wasn't refunded turns an Unsure checkout Good on By date; a refunded one turns it Cancelled. Bank data stays on this Mac.
