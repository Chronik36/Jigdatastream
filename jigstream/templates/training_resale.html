Resale products

Anything you buy to flip. A purchase that names one of these is an order wherever you bought it: Sam's Club, Aldi, a grocery or warehouse store, an in-store or pickup e-receipt. There's no store list; the product decides. On a receipt with other things too (groceries, household), only the resale items are kept, and the order's total is what they cost. Online orders stay whole.

Ads that had become orders

A store newsletter (a comic shop's "new this week" with Pokémon and One Piece packs and their prices) isn't an order. The AI used to read the prices next to each product as what you paid, which threw off totals. Now an email only becomes an order when it shows a real purchase: an order or confirmation number, a receipt, a payment, or shipping news. Orders saved before were checked once against their emails; the ones below showed none of that and were taken out. To teach the AI another one, open the order and pick Edit › What its emails are › Ad / newsletter, or press It's an ad.
