Products

Tell BotTrack what kind of product you're after. It searches what Target and Shopify stores list right now, with their real SKUs, prices and stock; then it looks up what they resell for on your selling sites and works out the profit: the going market price minus what it costs at each store. When stores charge different prices, all of them are listed and the cheapest one is recommended. Prices are looked up only when you click, with requests spaced out; a site that blocks automated visits is reported and you can type its price in.
