BotTrack
========

Install
1. Drag BotTrack into the Applications folder.
2. Open BotTrack. The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app for malware, because it
   isn't sold through Apple. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click
   "Open Anyway", and confirm. You only do this once.
3. The first launch gets the AI model ready (about 9 GB, downloaded once; if you already had it
   in Ollama, it's reused). The top of the window shows progress.
4. On the Setup page, add each Gmail inbox with an app password, and optionally connect Discord.
   The page explains both.

How it runs
Everything BotTrack uses is inside the app, including its own AI engine; nothing else needs
installing. While BotTrack is open it checks your inboxes (including Spam) and Discord channels
every 4 hours, and checks for app updates. Closing the window stops it.

Your data, settings, logs and the AI model are kept in one folder:
~/Library/Application Support/BotTrack
Your email is read only on this Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere except the connections to Gmail,
Discord and GitHub (for updates).

Updates: Setup > Updates has "Check for update" and "Update now", plus "Undo last update".

Remove: drag BotTrack from Applications to the Trash. To also delete your data and the AI model,
delete the folder above.

Moving from the old Order Tracker: just open BotTrack. It brings your inboxes, settings and order
history over and removes the old background service and shortcut by itself.
