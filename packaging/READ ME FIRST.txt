Order Tracker
=============

Install
1. Double-click "Install Order Tracker.command".
   If macOS says it can't be opened because it's from an unidentified developer:
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway",
   then double-click the installer again.
   (Or open Terminal, type "bash " and drag the installer into the window, then press Return.)
2. The installer sets everything up, downloads the AI model through Ollama (about 5 GB
   the first time), and opens Order Tracker in its own window. It's in your Applications
   folder and Spotlight; drag it to the Dock to keep it there.
3. On the Setup page, add each Gmail inbox with an app password (the page explains how).

4. Optional: on the same Setup page, connect Discord so your bots' checkout webhooks
   (and JSON posted in those channels) are read in. The page walks you through making a
   free Discord bot and picking the channels.

After that it runs by itself: it starts when you log in and checks your inboxes (including Spam)
and Discord channels every 4 hours. Open it from Spotlight by typing "Order Tracker".

Troubleshooting: the dashboard's Logs page shows what the app, each sync, the AI model and the
installer did, and "Download all logs" makes a zip you can send for help (app passwords are hidden).
The files are in ~/Library/Application Support/OrderTracker/logs.

Your email is read only on this Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere except the connection to Gmail.

Updates: Setup > Updates has "Check for update" and "Update now" buttons, and the app checks by
itself every 6 hours and shows a banner when a new version is ready. Updating keeps your inboxes,
settings and orders, and "Undo last update" puts the previous version back. While the code's
GitHub repository is private, the app needs a read-only GitHub token for this (Setup explains how).
Running a newer installer also works and keeps your data.
To remove it, double-click "Uninstall Order Tracker.command".
